Light Guard controls room lighting using motion sensors, door contacts and switches, with automations configured directly in the app's settings.

Motion lighting can keep a room softly lit after sunset instead of switching it off. Choose the idle brightness from 1 to 100 percent and optionally switch the lights off later, for example after three hours. New movement restores the motion brightness and resets the countdown. Lights switch off at sunrise. Night lighting is optional and starts disabled.

The room overview shows enabled, paused and disabled automation groups, together with recent events. It indicates the state of the automation, not whether the physical lights are currently on. The app also provides light groups, zone control, temporary overrides and Flow action cards.

To get started, open the app's settings, select your devices and create a room automation. Connection settings and help are available under Settings. Light Guard requires Homey Pro and access to Homey's devices through the Homey Web API. Sunrise and sunset are calculated locally from Homey's configured location.

Light Guard is Thomas Solvik's fork of Easy Automation by Finn Cato Andersen, incorporating the original's 0.1.14 fixes. It adds the Guard-style room overview, Light Guard branding and optional night-light behaviour. The interface also builds on House Guard and Power Guard; see NOTICE.md for attribution. The App Store edition has its own app ID and is installed separately from earlier development editions.
